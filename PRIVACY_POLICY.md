# Privacy Policy for Links to Meters Converter

**Effective Date:** 20/07/2025  
**Application:** Links to Meters Converter (`com.area.linkstometers`)

Thank you for using **Links to Meters Converter** ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our [Links to Meters Converter app on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.area.linkstometers).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We do **not** collect or store any personally identifiable information from users.

The app may request the following device permissions solely to provide core features:

* **Camera or Storage Access:** If you choose to scan a map image or land sketch for conversion, the app may access your device camera or gallery. All image processing and optical character recognition (OCR) is performed **locally on your device** and images are **never uploaded or stored** on external servers.
* **Internet Access:** If the app is used in online mode or to fetch calculations using the Gemini API, an internet connection is required. No personal user identifiers or private data are transmitted.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We only use data locally on your device to:
* Convert area land units (links, cents, gunthas, acres) into square meters and feet.
* Perform text recognition (OCR) from uploaded or scanned map images.

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with the following third-party service:
* **Google Gemini API** – for text analysis or intelligent unit conversion when online mode is active. This service operates under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Security

We are committed to keeping your information secure. Because all calculations and image conversions occur locally on your device and no personal accounts or cloud databases are maintained, user privacy risks are minimal.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
